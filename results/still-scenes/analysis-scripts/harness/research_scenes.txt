# The development scenes of the research track (step 0, frozen 17 October 2026), as SCENES_DIR/SCENE_ID from the
# repository root, built from folder names alone: the scenes of the scenes folders the runs of
# research_runs.txt use (82 lines), and every worked-example scene of the examples folders their config.yaml
# records (29 lines, at the end; a scene of a folder named `examples` is a worked example): 111 lines in all.
#
# The 82 lines are 68 scenes: data/dev_scenes/placeholder_first14, the scenes folder the s19_ph and s20_ph runs record, is a
# folder of 14 links to data/placeholder/eval/eval_00 to eval_13 (the first 14 placeholder scenes, as its name
# says), so each of those 14 scenes is listed twice, once under each folder. A run is replayed
# against the folder its config.yaml records, so both stay; a scene-level pass over the list takes each scene once
# with research_harness.distinct_scenes(), which resolves the links (the summary checks that the 68 and the 29 are
# also distinct by content). data/placeholder/eval also holds eval_14_audience_3, eval_15_audience_5,
# eval_20_no_one_lift and eval_21_no_one_screen, byte for byte the scenes of data/dev_scenes/dev_new/seed0/scenes; no listed
# run was evaluated on them there, so they are listed under dev_new/seed0 alone.
#
# The held-out placeholder scenes eval_16 to eval_19 (group_photo, lift_wait) are left out; eval_20_no_one_lift and
# eval_21_no_one_screen are development scenes and are kept. By its name no worked example is of a held-out
# situation, and research_harness.example() leaves out the truth of any episode of one.
# research_harness.py refuses to open a scene that is not on this list.
