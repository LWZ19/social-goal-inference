PART 1. Intra-layout correlation (ICC) of success, design effect for 14 layouts x 18 records
ICC(1) = (MSB - MSW) / (MSB + (m - 1) MSW), one-way ANOVA by layout, m = 18 records per layout, 14 layouts, n = 252 records per cell (stats.icc_oneway; cross-checked by an independent numpy version).
DEFF = 1 + 17 ICC; n_eff = 252 / DEFF (records worth of independent information). Boot = cluster bootstrap over layouts, 2,000 draws, numpy default_rng(2), percentile 95%; it is rough with 14 clusters.
ICC_item = ICC(1) by item (arm, scene, episode; 84 items x 3 trials): the share of variance between items; 1 - ICC_item is the share that is trial-to-trial (sampling) noise.
