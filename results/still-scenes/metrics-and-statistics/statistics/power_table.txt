PART 3. Power of a sealed set of n fresh layouts, new wording, all six models
Design (stats.py Design A): draw n layouts with replacement from the 14 observed integer layout differences (sum over 18 paired records), apply the exact sign-flip test by layout (two-sided, statistic = sum), reject at p <= 0.05; 10,000 draws per cell; numpy default_rng((0, model index, contrast index)). MC SE <= 0.005.
POST-HOC AND OPTIMISTIC: it resamples the observed effects of the same 14 layouts that showed the effect (no shrinkage for selection), assumes fresh layouts mix situations as these do (6 nobody-near, 5 lines, 3 groups), that new situations behave like the old ones, and that the run noise of the fresh run equals that of these runs.

Why n = 8 is weak: two-sided exact sign-flip p >= 2 / 2^k with k non-tied layouts, so p <= 0.05 needs k >= 6. With 6 of 14 layouts usually tied (nobody-near), 8 fresh layouts give about 4.6 non-tied on average.

Per-situation power (for I46 kill: "more than 40 layouts needed per situation"): fresh layouts of one situation only, drawn from that situation's observed layout differences (lines: 5 layouts, groups: 3). Degenerate by construction: where every observed layout difference of a situation is positive, any resample of n >= 6 layouts is all positive and p = 2 / 2^n <= 0.05, so power is 1. Where one of 3 group layouts is 0, power follows the chance of drawing at least 6 non-zero layouts. This shows only that the kill line does not fire under this design; it says nothing about how fresh layouts of a situation will vary.
