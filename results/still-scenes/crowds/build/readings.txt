   attempt, k = number of people the family adds, taken = the base figures' colours); then the family's draws:
   - A: the facing sign s (recipes._sign), then up to 200 recipes._person calls (each draws the +-0.05 m, +-0.15 rad
     jitter) until c1, c2, c3, c5, c6 pass;
   - B: per draw, bearing theta ~ U(-pi, pi), turn psi ~ U(0, 2pi/3), then for each of the 3 people a yaw ~ U(-pi, pi)

2. B and C bisection: done before the jitter, as the note orders ("bisect ..., then apply the jitter and checks").
   The root taken is the outermost one along the bearing (the smallest distance beyond which the nearest corner-member
   distance stays at or above the gap), found by a 0.02 m scan from the centroid out to 30 m and bisection to 1e-10 m.
   With one cluster or group per variant, "20 restarts x 200 draws" is 4,000 draws of that one unit.
3. D: one "position draw" is one uniform sample of the band [1.2, 2.5] m (distance to the nearest member), made by
   rejection inside the members' bounding box grown by 2.5 m (at most 10,000 tries). The band shapes the draw and is
   applied before the jitter; the spacing rule (>= 1.2 m from every person) and c1-c8 are applied to the jittered
   person. People are placed one after another; a person with no spot in 200 draws restarts the whole set (20 times).
4. B and C spacing (>= 1.2 m from every other non-member already in the scene) and every pre-check are applied to the
   jittered people.
5. c5 samples each line of sight every 0.05 m from the robot's ground position, as recipes._in_clear_view does
   (recipes._sight_line). For c5(b), "present" is the base's people plus the people of the variant placed earlier in
   the same attempt (D places one after another; B and C place one unit, whose members are covered by each other's
   c5(a)).

   position = the translation of T_world_camera).

   person's line of sight clear.
