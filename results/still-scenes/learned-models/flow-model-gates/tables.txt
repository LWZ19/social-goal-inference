Definitions. S = mean over an episode's region cells of log q (nats per region cell), then the mean over episodes. M = sum of q over the region cells; lift = M / (n_region / N_floor); both means of per-episode values. q mixes 1% uniform into every density method; band = 0.99 uniform on floor cells with d_t in [q05, q95] of the training region cells; uniform = 1 / N_floor. e0 and e1 of a scene share one region set and one q, so they carry identical values; n is given as episodes and scenes.
