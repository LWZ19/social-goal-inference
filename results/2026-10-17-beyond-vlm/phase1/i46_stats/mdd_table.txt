PART 5. Minimum detectable differences (MDD) for live A/B runs: 80% power, two-sided alpha 0.05
Normal approximation, MDD = (z0.975 + z0.80) x SD of the difference = 2.802 x SD. Records of 252 (pp = /2.52).

M1. Two independent live batches (A and B each one 3-trial batch, nothing shared): SD diff = sqrt(2) x batch SD.

  batch SD 6                        =  6.00: MDD  23.8 records ( 9.4 pp)
  batch SD 7                        =  7.00: MDD  27.7 records (11.0 pp)
